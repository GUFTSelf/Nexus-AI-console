## Information processed

Depending on the enabled features, Nexus AI may process:

- text, claims, evidence, or deterministic instructions submitted by the user;
- account information supplied through Google or email authentication;
- verification results, canonical execution records, and audit metadata;
- limited device, crash, security, and performance information supplied by configured platform services;
- subscription and entitlement status when paid plans are enabled.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide verification, deterministic execution, replay, authentication, account management, security, support, and service improvement. Nexus AI does not treat advertising profiling or sale of personal information as part of its stated core purpose.

## Deterministic execution

Offline deterministic execution is designed to run on the device. Its canonical SHA-256 commitment excludes identity, timestamps, random audit identifiers, and device metadata.

## AI and third-party processing

When AI features are enabled, submitted content may be sent through the Nexus protected backend to the configured AI provider. The final policy must identify the actual providers, purposes, retention settings, and international-transfer terms used in production. A production mobile binary must not contain the provider's secret API credential.

Authentication and operational services may include Google Firebase and Apple platform services when configured. Their processing is governed by the applicable service terms and the final Nexus production configuration.

## Storage and retention

Verification cases and audit records may be stored locally on the device. Cloud retention periods must be defined before cloud synchronization or enterprise workspaces are released. Information should be retained only as long as needed for the stated purpose, security, contractual obligations, and applicable law.

## User controls

Users can delete individual verification cases. Signed-in Android users can request account deletion in the authentication panel, which also clears locally stored verification cases and audit records after successful account deletion. Equivalent deletion behavior must be completed and verified on iOS before account creation is enabled there.

## Security

Nexus AI uses platform security controls, access restrictions, and cryptographic integrity commitments. No system can guarantee absolute security. A monitored security contact and incident-response procedure must be published before launch.

## Children

The initial release is not directed to children. The final store age rating and any age-gating requirements must match the production functionality and target audience.

## Changes

Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, where required, providing notice through the application or service.
